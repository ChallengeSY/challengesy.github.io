Instructions

Jumbo is a close relative to Relaxed Golf. They both have a single foundation that builds up and down by rank, allowing King-Ace wraparound. Jumbo also has the perk of introducing 2 jokers into the mixture. These cards serve as any rank (akin to a wildcard), opening up options and also slightly increasing the stock size.
